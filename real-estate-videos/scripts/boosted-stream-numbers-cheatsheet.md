## رقم الخوف (the fear is real — say it first, own it)
- H1 2026 residential sales: −14% volume, −16% value vs H1 2025 (Anarock via Business Standard)
- ValuStrat index: ~−10% dip Feb→June 2026 after regional shock, June stabilized (−1% MoM, sales +33.5% MoM)
- Rents: −6.2% Q1→Q2 2026 citywide (CBRE via Gulf News); JVC −10.3%, Burj Khalifa district −10.2%, JBR −9.9% (Time Out 2026)
- Fitch warning: up to −15% correction risk, ~120K units scheduled 2026 · S&P counter-view: no broad oversupply, stress case −5–10% only in oversupplied segments
- BUT: 2025 full year was a record — 270K+ transactions, AED 917bn (DLD) · prices +9.8% (DLD index) · June 2026 volumes rebounded +33.5% MoM (ValuStrat)
- Realistic supply: only ~46–48% of scheduled units actually deliver on time (Knight Frank/Moody's rates); H1 2026 actual: ~18K units

## فين تاخد بالك (based on numbers, not hate)
- Saturation flags (concurrent handovers): JVC, Arjan, Business Bay, Dubailand corridor (Betterhomes/Westgate 2026) — JVC rents already −10.3%
- Service-charge trap: Downtown AED 11–68/sqft vs International City 6–10, JVC 8–14, villas 2–6 (Driven Properties Index 2026). On 1,000 sqft: AED 12 vs 40/sqft = AED 28K/year difference — eats 2+ points of yield
- Off-plan delays: ~40–50% of projects delayed; tier-1 developers ~3–5 months, small developers 12–18 months (industry data — say «حسب بيانات السوق») — developer track record is the whole game
- Business Bay: rent star (+18.2% YoY Q4 2025) but expensive entry (studios AED 1.2M+) — it's a rent/capital story, not a cheap-yield story
- 2027 = biggest actual delivery year in a decade (~70K units) — pick areas where demand drivers are real
