## SLIDE 2 — Developer

**Developer — Al Tarrad Development**

Al Tarrad is a Dubai developer working a focused, repeatable formula: mid-rise residential
towers in Dubailand aimed squarely at the investor and entry-end-user market — fully
furnished, smart-home fitted, priced below the city's mid-market. **Celesto Tower 4 is the
fourth in a numbered series**, which tells you the model is proven enough for them to keep
running it.

The design package itself is a genuine confidence signal, and worth a line to the client:
this isn't a marketing render pack. It carries a full climate and solar study, site
accessibility and land-use analysis, verified area calculations against DDA-allowed GFA,
a reconciled unit matrix and complete stamped floor plans. The residential GFA is
**22,309.05 sqm against an allowed 22,311.00 sqm — 99.99% of the permitted envelope.**

> **Be straight about what that last number means.** The developer has extracted essentially
> every square metre the plot allows. That is efficient, and it is also why there are 414
> units on a plot of roughly 3,100 sqm. Density is the deal here — it drives the low entry
> price, and it drives the competition at handover. Both, not one.

**Verify:** Al Tarrad's delivery record on Celesto 1–3 — how many completed, and on time.
That is the single most useful piece of diligence on this project and I don't have it.

## Where Celesto 4 sits against the other two

| | Floareá Skies | Arancia | **Celesto 4** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Location | JVC | City of Arabia | **DLRC / Wadi Al Safa 5** |
| Handover | Q3–Q4 2027 | Q1 2029 | **Q4 2028** |
| Total units | 192 | 272 | **414** |
| 1BR size | 717 sqft | 750–762 sqft | **550–598 sqft** |
| 1BR all-in | ~AED 1,055,250 | ~AED 1,045,250 | **~AED 837,250 (est)** |
| Price/sqft | AED 1,464 | AED 1,333 | **AED 1,454 (est)** |
| Net yield | 6.2% | 6.1% | **6.2% / 6.6% studio** |
| Capital pre-handover | 50% | 40% | **50%, but 1%/month** |
| Furnished | Yes | No | **Yes** |
| Metro | Purple Line (future) | — | **Blue Line, 1 min (future)** |
| DXB | 35 min | CONFIRM | **18 min** |

**Where Celesto genuinely wins:**
- **The cheapest way in by a wide margin** — a studio all-in at ~AED 567k, roughly half the
  entry of the other two. Different budget tier entirely.
- **The payment plan is the best of the three for cash flow.** 1% monthly for 30 months is
  far gentler than a lump milestone schedule — the client funds it out of income, not capital.
- **Best connectivity, and it isn't close.** 18 minutes to Downtown, DIFC *and* the airport,
  plus a Blue Line station at 1 minute. Floareá's Metro catalyst is a Purple Line station;
  this one is nearer and on a line already under construction.
- **Furnished** like Floareá, so it leases fast.
- **Highest yield of the three** on the studio at 6.6%.

**Where it loses, and this needs saying plainly:**
- **414 units in one building** — more than double Floareá's 192. At handover the client's
  1BR competes with 179 identical neighbours, and 162 studios hit the rental market at the
  same time. This is the project's real weakness, and it is structural, not fixable.
- **The 1BR is small — 550 sqft against 717 and 750.** Price/sqft is *not* cheap
  (AED 1,454, essentially the same as Floareá's 1,464). The low ticket comes from a smaller
  box, not a discount. Do not let the client believe otherwise; they'll discover it at
  viewing and lose trust in the rest of the pitch.
- **39% of the building is studios** — an investor-heavy tenant profile, higher churn,
  weaker owner-occupier base at resale.
- **One parking space per apartment** and no resident surplus.
- **Least established developer of the three** and no verified delivery record in hand.
- **Inland Dubailand** — no view premium, no waterfront.

**How I'd frame the three for the client:**
- **Celesto 4** — lowest entry, easiest payment plan, best transport. Buy the *location's
  future*, accept the density.
- **Floareá Skies** — earliest handover, fitted, proven JVC leasing market. Buy the *income
  starting soonest*.
- **Arancia** — strongest developer and the only true community product. Buy the *asset
  quality*, wait until 2029.

That's three genuinely different propositions rather than three variations of the same one —
which is what makes this a real decision instead of a ranking.

4. Escrow account and RERA/DDA project registration.
