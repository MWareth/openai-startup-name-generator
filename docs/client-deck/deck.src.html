What each one costs you

No project here is perfect

Every option has a real weakness. These are the ones worth knowing before signing, not after.

Floareá Skies

The catch
  • Boutique developer with a thin delivery record — the real risk on this deal
  • JVC is Dubai's most crowded pipeline: 16,800–22,000 units to 2028, so rents may soften 5–10% right at handover
  • 70% falls due on completion — the largest single payment of the three, usually the point a mortgage is drawn
  • The highest all-in cost of the three at AED 1.2M, and on my modelling the lowest net yield at 5.7%
  • Service charge is the highest of the three at AED 15/sqft — the rooftop pool and meditation deck cost something to run

Arancia

The catch
  • Q1 2029 handover — the longest wait. Four years before a dirham of rent
  • 1,560 units across the masterplan: your client's resale competes with the developer's own later phases at newer stock
  • Inland City of Arabia — resale depends on the community delivering as drawn
  • Not furnished, unlike the other two
  • The only one with no published service charge, and the heaviest amenity and landscape load of the three

Celesto Tower 4

The catch
  • 414 units in one building — more than double Floareá. At handover the 1BR competes with 179 identical neighbours
  • 39% of the building is studios — investor-heavy, higher churn, weaker owner-occupier base at resale
  • The 1BR is small at 550 sqft; price per foot is not cheap
  • One parking space per apartment, no resident surplus
  • Least established developer, delivery record on Celesto 1–3 unverified

The decision

Three different propositions, not a ranking

The numbers land close enough that they don't decide this. What decides it is which of these three sentences sounds most like you.

“I want the biggest apartment, the earliest keys, and the least money tied up until then.”
Floareá Skies
Q1 2028, 837 sqft, and only 30% before handover. The trade is a lower yield and a 70% completion payment.
“I can wait. I want the strongest name behind it and the cheapest land per foot.”
Arancia
OMNIYAT's premium brand at AED 1,333/sqft, and the only true community product of the three.
“I want the smallest cheque, paid monthly, and the best yield.”
Celesto Tower 4
Lowest entry by a wide margin, 1% a month for 30 months, the strongest modelled yield, and metro at one minute.
My read, and it changed once the real Floareá numbers came in: on a live sales offer Floareá prices at AED 1.2M all-in for 837 sqft — the largest unit and the earliest keys, but the lowest modelled yield at 5.7%. So for a yield-first buyer the answer is now Celesto 4 — and its lead widened once the published service charges came in (12/sqft against Floareá's 15). For a buyer who wants space, the earliest handover and the least capital tied up, it is Floareá. What would change it again: Celesto's 1BR price is still unconfirmed — if it lands above AED 900,000 its yield advantage largely disappears and Arancia takes it.
