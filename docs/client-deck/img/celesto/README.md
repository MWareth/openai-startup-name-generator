## Art direction
Celesto's own look is **cream and gold curved balcony bands on blue glass**, with a dark
navy fluted podium — lighter and more "classic-glam" than Villa Del Garda's dark cinematic
treatment. Same recommendation as Floareá: keep the deck's unified dark treatment for
cohesion and let the render carry the project's identity.
