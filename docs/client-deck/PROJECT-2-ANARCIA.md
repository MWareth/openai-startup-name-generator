## SLIDE 2 — Developer

**Developer — BEYOND**

BEYOND is the premium residential brand of **OMNIYAT Group**, founded in Dubai in 2005 —
the group behind Dubai's ultra-prime waterfront portfolio. BEYOND applies that
design-and-delivery pedigree to master-planned community living rather than single towers.

The Yards is BEYOND's **AED 4 billion (US $1.09bn)** master-planned destination in City of
Arabia: **2.3 million sqft** of gross floor area, **1,560 residences**, organised along a
**1-kilometre green spine**, with **70% of the total area given to open landscape**. The
masterplan is Mediterranean-inspired and includes its own school, clinic, mosque, farm,
gardens and retail — a complete community, not an apartment block with a pool.

**Arancia is the first residential cluster to launch** within it: **272 residences** across
**three low-rise buildings (G+6 and G+7)** arranged around a central green valley. Low
density and a named-phase position inside a much larger masterplan — the buyer is early
into a community that will still be under construction around them for years, which is
both the upside (entry pricing) and the risk (delivery timeline).

### ⚠️ Two rows are ASSUMED — fix before this goes to the client
1. **Service charge — AED 15/sqft is my assumption**, not a published figure. Arancia's
   service charge isn't disclosed anywhere public. AED 15/sqft is a typical Dubailand
   low-rise rate, but the amenity load here is heavy (lagoon pool, spa, cinema, farm),

## Positioning against a sea-view tower (for the head-to-head slide)

Arancia is the **community and greenery** play, not the address play. Where it genuinely wins:

- **Entry capital.** ~AED 1.05M all-in vs ~AED 2.93M for the Villa Del Garda-style 1BR —
  roughly **one third the cheque for a comparable net yield**. That is the headline.
- **Payment plan.** 40/60 with milestones stretched to 2028 — only 20% due before
  September 2027. Cash stays with the client far longer.
- **Density.** 272 units in the phase, low-rise, G+6/G+7 — real scarcity within the cluster.
- **Masterplan spend.** AED 4bn committed around them, with school, clinic and retail
  in-plan — supports end-user demand at handover, not just investor churn.

Where it loses, and say so plainly:

- **Handover Q1 2029** — the longest wait of the three. Four years of construction risk and
  four years before a dirham of rent.
- **1,560 units coming across the masterplan** — the client's exit competes with the later
  phases of the same developer, at newer stock and possibly better pricing.
- **Location is inland Dubailand**, not waterfront. No view premium, and resale depends on
  the community delivering as drawn.
- **Service charge unknown** on a heavily amenitised scheme — the one figure that could
  quietly eat the yield.
