# Fitness & Wellness Campaign — Brief

Dubai's fitness crowd, met where they actually are: wanting something sweet that fits the routine. Hero is the **Healthy Carrot Balls** (energy bites); the rest of the menu plays the "balance / rest day" role. Meta, Send Message objective, Dubai-targeted, mute-first — same delivery playbook as world-cup.

## Three-shot structure (every cut)
1. **Warm-up** — motion, intent, morning-light energy. Item enters the routine (gym bag, kitchen counter pre-workout, yoga mat rolled out).
2. **Peak** — the hero beat. Item held mid-frame like a rep at the top — clean, controlled, brand-brown & cream framing.
3. **Refuel** — close-up payoff. The bite, the texture, the order CTA.

## Mandatory on every cut
- **First 1–2s:** brand-brown + cream frame, logo + item name visible.
- **Every shot:** super in NF One Little Font Bold (ad) / Lemonade (video).
- **End card:** `DM to order 💪 @deestreats.ae` + Send Message prompt.
- **Style:** medium-low motion (Kling primary), clean morning light, warm. Cohesive across all cuts.

## Legal & claims — keep it puffery, not nutrition
- **No nutrition claims** we can't back on a label: no "protein-packed", "sugar-free", "low-cal", "keto". "Naturally sweet" and "wholesome" are fine — that's the existing product language.
- **No real athletes or trainers** (same rule as world-cup), no gym brand logos or recognizable studio interiors. Generic: "gym bag", "yoga mat", "running shoes", "resistance band".
- Nothing that implies medical or weight-loss benefit.

## The three cuts

> Supers below are starting hooks — 2–3 variants each on hooks/CTAs, middle lines lock early.

### 1. Healthy Carrot Balls — the hero
Pre/post-workout fuel. This is the cut that carries the campaign.
- **Warm-up:** carrot balls dropped into a gym bag pocket, morning light. Hook variants:
  - *"Fuel that tastes like a treat."*
  - *"The snack your trainer won't confiscate."*
  - *"Sweet. Wholesome. Zero drama."*
- **Peak:** one bite held up clean against cream backdrop. Super: *"Naturally sweet energy bites."*
- **Refuel:** the bite, texture visible. CTA: *"DM to order 💪"*

### 2. Carrot Cake Muffins — the wholesome middle
The bridge product — feels like a treat, reads like breakfast.
- **Warm-up:** muffin next to rolled yoga mat, kettle steam. Hook variants:
  - *"Earned it before 8am."*
  - *"Post-workout, pre-everything."*
- **Peak:** cream cheese top, lifted slow. Super: *"Spiced. Soft. Deserved."*
- **Refuel:** the crumb pull. CTA: *"Send a message to order."*

### 3. Rest Day — San Sebastian (the balance play)
Speaks to the same audience on their day off. Balance, not cheat-day guilt.
- **Warm-up:** running shoes by the door, untouched. Hook variants:
  - *"Rest days count too."*
  - *"You don't earn dessert. You just order it."*
- **Peak:** the burnt top, glowing, trophy energy carried over from world-cup.
- **Refuel:** spoon pulls the creamy center. CTA: *"DM @deestreats.ae 💪"*
